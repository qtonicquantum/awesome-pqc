# Editorial Criteria

This list is curated. The bar for inclusion is intentionally narrow.

## What Belongs

- **Standards and specifications** published by recognized bodies: NIST, IETF, ETSI, ISO, BSI, ANSSI, NCSC.
- **Government and policy** documents from national security or cybersecurity agencies.
- **Libraries and implementations** that are open source, actively maintained, and primarily focused on post-quantum cryptography or include first-class PQC support.
- **Tools and scanners** that materially help inventory or assess cryptographic posture relevant to PQ migration.
- **CBOM and cryptographic inventory** specifications and reference material.
- **Research and papers** from canonical project pages or the IACR ePrint archive.
- **Test vectors** from standards bodies or well-known cross-vendor projects.
- **Migration guides** from cloud providers, standards bodies, or national agencies.
- **Quantum threat timeline** sources from established research institutes.
- **Conferences and communities** with stable URLs and a substantial PQC focus.
- **Educational** materials that are free, complete, and from credible institutions.
- **Vendor and industry** pages that are canonical product or program landing pages, not marketing campaigns.

## What Does Not Belong

- Personal blog posts unless authored by a recognized maintainer of the underlying project.
- Vendor pages that exist primarily to capture leads.
- Tutorials hosted on link-shortened or ad-heavy domains.
- Resources whose URL canonical form cannot be verified.
- Resources whose primary value is marketing rather than technical or policy substance.

## Editorial Voice

- One sentence per entry, factual, ending in a period.
- No editorial superlatives ("best", "amazing", "must-have").
- No public pricing references.
- No customer logos.
- "Qtonic Quantum" is always the full form; "Intelligence Model" is preferred over the term commonly used to describe machine learning systems.

## Link Stability

URLs must resolve over HTTPS without redirects through tracking domains. Prefer canonical paths under csrc.nist.gov, rfc-editor.org, datatracker.ietf.org, cyclonedx.org, and other well-known organizational domains.
